
FIELDSHIFT - TECHNICAL METHODOLOGY
NASA SPACE APPS CHALLENGE 2026

1. PROJECT PURPOSE

FieldShift is a farm decision-support prototype designed to help
farmers explore crop rotation alternatives using environmental
observations and agricultural suitability information.

The initial demonstration area is around Jasra Organic Farm,
Bahrain. The location is a demonstration reference point and
has not been confirmed as the actual cultivated field boundary.

2. ENVIRONMENTAL DATA

NASA POWER daily climate observations for 2025 were used to
analyse maximum and minimum temperature, relative humidity,
precipitation and solar radiation.

Solar radiation is reported as an average daily value in
kWh/m2/day. Annual rainfall is calculated by summing daily
precipitation estimates.

GPM IMERG V07 satellite precipitation estimates were extracted
for 2025 and compared with NASA POWER rainfall estimates.
The two datasets showed substantial differences. Both estimates
are retained for comparison, and the discrepancy requires
validation against reliable ground observations.

3. SENTINEL-2 VEGETATION ANALYSIS

Sentinel-2 Level-2A imagery was used to calculate the
Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI).

The red (B04) and near-infrared (B08) bands were used.
The Scene Classification Layer (SCL) was applied to mask
invalid, cloud, cirrus, shadow, snow and unclassified pixels.
The SCL layer was aligned to the red-band grid using
nearest-neighbour resampling before NDVI calculation.

The analysis uses a small search area around the demonstration
coordinates, not a verified farm polygon. The resulting NDVI
is therefore an indicative vegetation measure for the search
area, not a confirmed measurement of crop health or farm
productivity. Residual classification errors may remain.

4. AGRICULTURAL DATA

FAO GAEZ crop suitability information for Bahrain was used
to compare irrigated and rain-fed suitability scenarios.

The GAEZ data used is at national geographic level and should
not be interpreted as field-level suitability.

A reference database of 52 crops was prepared, including
botanical families, rotation groups and crop characteristics.

Eight annual crops were selected for the initial rotation
prototype. Heat-tolerance, water-demand and growing-season
categories were assigned as provisional screening labels and
require independent agronomic validation.

5. ROTATION GENERATION

Three-crop candidate sequences were generated using the
selected annual crops.

The prototype required:
- Three different botanical families.
- At least one legume.
- No repeated botanical family in the cyclic sequence,
  including the transition from the final crop back to the first.

Circular duplicates were removed, resulting in 42 unique
ordered circular sequences and 21 unique crop combinations.

Alternative orders are retained for each crop combination.

6. PROVISIONAL SCORING

The prototype uses the following weighted screening factors:
- GAEZ favourable suitability: 40 percent.
- GAEZ very suitable extent: 15 percent.
- Heat-tolerance category: 25 percent.
- Water-demand category: 20 percent.

Favourable suitability and very suitable extent are normalized
against the maximum value among the eight selected crops.

Heat and water categories are converted into numerical scores
using provisional manually assigned category values.

The overall score is calculated from the average component
scores of the three crops in a rotation.

IMPORTANT SCORING LIMITATION:
The current score evaluates the average crop-level
characteristics of a three-crop combination. It does not
evaluate crop-to-crop interactions, soil nutrient effects,
pest and disease breaks, or the agronomic effect of planting
one crop before another.

Consequently, alternative orders of the same three crops
receive the same score. The score is a provisional screening
indicator, not a sequence-specific agronomic evaluation,
yield prediction or probability of successful cultivation.

7. RECOMMENDATION OUTPUT

The prototype ranks 21 unique crop combinations and provides
42 alternative ordered sequences.

The grouped recommendation output is intended for the
interface. Its explanation describes the crop combination,
while the alternative sequences are listed separately.

The detailed rotation output contains individual sequences
and their component scores.

8. LIMITATIONS AND VALIDATION REQUIREMENTS

- Scores are prototype screening scores, not yield predictions
  or probabilities of successful cultivation.
- Heat-tolerance, water-demand and growing-season categories
  require agronomic validation.
- The scoring model does not evaluate planting-order effects
  or crop-to-crop interactions.
- Local planting calendars and crop sequence timing require
  validation.
- The NDVI search area is not a verified farm boundary.
- Sentinel-2 SCL masking reduces cloud and shadow contamination,
  but residual classification errors may remain.
- GPM rainfall estimates differed substantially from NASA POWER
  and require validation against ground observations.
- The selected GAEZ national rain-fed scenario reported no
  favourable suitability for the 52 crops. This does not prove
  that rain-fed cultivation is impossible.
- GAEZ national-level suitability does not establish suitability
  for an individual farm or field.
- Irrigation availability, water quality, soil conditions and
  actual farm management practices have not been assessed.
- The original GAEZ source workbooks and external data access
  dependencies are not included in the output CSV package.

9. REPRODUCIBILITY

The analysis notebook contains the extraction and processing
workflow. Re-running the complete workflow may require the
original GAEZ source workbooks, internet access, Sentinel-2
data access, and Google Earth Engine authentication and
project configuration.

The CSV files in the handover package are exported results
for use by the interface and project team.

10. INTENDED USE

FieldShift provides exploratory crop rotation options to
support discussion and further investigation.

It does not replace local agronomic advice, soil testing,
irrigation assessment or verified farm-level suitability
analysis.
